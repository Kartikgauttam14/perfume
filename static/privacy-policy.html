Mansam Perfume · Concierge & WhatsApp

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy describes the Mansam Perfume concierge website at perfume-p6gi.onrender.com and its connected WhatsApp automation (Meta app: Mansam Perfume). It explains what information these services process and how to request its deletion. It does not replace the policies of separate shopping websites or Meta/WhatsApp.

Information we process

  • Website conversations: messages you type, recent conversation history, your selected language, fragrance preferences, and feedback you submit about an answer.
  • Advisor requests: the name, phone number, city or boutique preference, and any other details you choose to provide when requesting a concierge handoff.
  • WhatsApp: incoming messages and associated identifiers, phone numbers, timestamps, and delivery information provided through Meta’s WhatsApp API. The Supabase automation stores incoming message IDs, recipient numbers, timestamps, reply states, and outgoing message IDs. It does not store message text in that table.
  • Technical information: hosting and messaging providers may process IP addresses, request information, device/browser information, and operational logs to run and protect their services.

Please do not send payment-card information, passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information through the concierge chat.

How information is used

We process information to answer fragrance questions, provide recommendations, handle requested advisor handoffs, respond to messages, investigate errors and feedback, and prevent duplicate WhatsApp replies. The WhatsApp automation can send the approved shop_now product template after a new incoming customer message. Automated recommendations may be inaccurate; contact an advisor for assistance.

Service providers and sharing

The website is hosted by Render. WhatsApp messages are processed through Meta/WhatsApp, and the WhatsApp webhook and reply records are hosted by Supabase. Website chat prompts and recent conversation history may be sent to OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider to generate an answer. The current WhatsApp template-reply function does not send WhatsApp message content to OpenRouter.

Optional speech recognition and speech playback may use your browser’s services. If the website’s text-to-speech service is used, text for playback is sent to Google’s text-to-speech endpoint. Advisors may receive information you choose to share during a handoff. Providers process data under their own applicable policies and may process information in countries outside your own.

Provider information: WhatsApp, Supabase, Render, OpenRouter, and Google.

Browser storage and optional features

The website stores language and audio preferences in your browser’s local storage. You can remove them through your browser’s site-data settings. Speech input requires microphone permission; you can decline or revoke that permission and use text instead.

Storage, retention, and security

WhatsApp reply records are stored in a private Supabase database table with row-level security and no public-user access. Server credentials are stored as secrets, and the webhook verifies Meta’s signatures. Website feedback can create persisted correction records. Hosting and provider logs may have their own retention periods.

The current WhatsApp integration does not automatically expire reply records. Records remain until removed by the operator or through a deletion request. We cannot promise that any online system is completely secure. Provider backups or records required for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes may remain after active records are deleted.

Your choices and data deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use of this service, or ask to stop promotional replies. Requests are handled by a person; sending a keyword to the bot is not an automatic deletion mechanism.

To request deletion: email customercare@mansamworld.com with the subject “Mansam Perfume data deletion request.”

Include the WhatsApp number you used (with country code), or enough information to identify the relevant website conversation or advisor request. Do not include a password, access token, or identity document. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.

Specify whether you want WhatsApp reply records, website feedback records, or other information associated with this service deleted. You can also ask a human advisor in your existing WhatsApp conversation for privacy assistance. Requests about records held independently by Meta or another provider should also be directed to that provider.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions about the Mansam Perfume concierge and WhatsApp service, contact customercare@mansamworld.com. If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published on this page.
